What the certificate assumes

The heading law is an immediate fixed-rate shortest turn, followed by straight continuation. The speed bounds and declared heading, position and relative-velocity errors must hold throughout the checked interval. Route turns and response delays are outside this model.

With vertical separation enabled, each aircraft progresses towards its selected level at or above the declared minimum rate and remains there. Levels have no altitude-error allowance. The plotted bands are unrounded; the verified resolution time also respects the selected rounding grid.

The relative view uses the same constant-speed hull and the same maximum allowance as the solver. Turning cases can enlarge the narrower speed interval to enclose both declarations. The numerical kernel has an empirical floating-point guard, not a proved error bound for all inputs.
